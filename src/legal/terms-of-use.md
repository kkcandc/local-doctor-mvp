4. Telehealth Services and Limitations

Telehealth allows patients and Providers to communicate remotely using electronic technologies.

Although telehealth improves access to specialty healthcare, it has limitations, including:

Inability to conduct certain physical or neurological examinations remotely.

Potential technical interruptions or communication failures.

Providers may recommend in-person care or determine that telehealth is unsuitable for an individual patient.

Telehealth services are not substitutes for emergency medical attention or medically necessary in-person care.
